Partnership Information

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I become a Parusavedhi dealer?

Contact our partnerships team with your business information, location and intended market. Potential dealership arrangements are subject to discussion, evaluation and mutual agreement.

Is there a minimum investment requirement?

Investment requirements depend on the proposed equipment, commercial arrangement and business model. Contact our team for applicable information.

Do you offer exclusive distribution territories?

Territory arrangements, distribution rights and commercial conditions are subject to individual discussion and written agreement.

Can I start a business as an individual?

We welcome enquiries from aspiring entrepreneurs and independent food vendors. Our team can discuss suitable equipment options and commercial requirements.

Can self-help groups contact Parusavedhi?

Yes. We welcome preliminary discussions with self-help groups, community enterprises and organizations interested in food-based entrepreneurial opportunities.

Is Parusavedhi currently exporting internationally?

International expansion forms part of our long-term vision. Prospective international partners may contact our exports enquiry channel to discuss future opportunities and applicable requirements.

Responsible Business Relationships

All partnership opportunities described on this website are invitations for preliminary business discussions.

Partnership availability, pricing, territorial rights, investment requirements, product supply, technical support and other commercial conditions are subject to applicable requirements and mutually agreed terms.

No business arrangement or financial outcome is guaranteed by submitting an enquiry.
