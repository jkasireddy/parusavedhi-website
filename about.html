Who We Are

A Simple Thought. A Greater Purpose.

Parusavedhi Food Industries Private Limited is an Indian food technology and manufacturing enterprise established in 2021.

Our founding vision emerged from a simple belief: nutritious, affordable and convenient food choices should be accessible to everyone.

By combining traditional food preferences with modern engineering, we seek to develop practical food preparation solutions that benefit consumers, businesses and communities.

Our current product portfolio includes electrically operated corn roasting machines and sweet corn boiling machines, supported by our manufacturing, processing, distribution and servicing capabilities.

Why We Exist

Innovation Begins With a Human Need.

The inspiration behind Parusavedhi began years before its formal incorporation, with an observation about the food choices available in everyday life.

Convenience often influences what people eat. Our founding idea was to explore food preparation methods that could make nutritious and satisfying alternatives easier to access and more convenient to consume.

Corn, a familiar and widely enjoyed food, became an opportunity to bring this idea to life through engineering and technology.

Rather than viewing traditional food vendors as competitors, we see opportunities to support entrepreneurs, retailers and local businesses through practical food preparation equipment.

No positive change is too small.
