Electric Corn Roaster

The Parusavedhi Electric Corn Roasting Machine is an electrically operated food preparation solution developed for roasting fresh corn on the cob.

The machine brings together traditional food preferences and modern heating technology, offering an alternative to conventional charcoal-based roasting methods.

It reflects our commitment to practical innovation, convenience and food-based entrepreneurial opportunities.
