Privacy Policy

Information about how we handle personal and business enquiry data.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Parusavedhi Food Industries Private Limited ("Parusavedhi", "we", "us" or "our") handles information collected through its corporate website, www.parusavedhi.com.

We seek to handle personal information responsibly and transparently.

2. Information We Collect

When you contact us or submit an enquiry, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name and email address.
  • Telephone number, if provided.
  • Company or organization name.
  • City, state and country.
  • Product and business interests.
  • Enquiry details and communications.

Our hosting and security service providers may also process technical information, including IP addresses, browser information, request metadata and security signals.

3. How We Use Information

Information provided through our website may be used for purposes including:

  • Responding to enquiries.
  • Providing product information and quotations.
  • Discussing business partnerships.
  • Providing customer and technical assistance.
  • Maintaining business communication records.
  • Protecting website functionality and security.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations.

Submission of an enquiry does not automatically subscribe you to promotional communications.

4. Website Hosting and Service Providers

Our website uses Cloudflare services for hosting, network delivery, security and automated abuse prevention.

Cloudflare Turnstile may process technical information to distinguish legitimate visitors from automated abuse.

Our website may load fonts through Google Fonts, which can involve technical information being transmitted to Google's servers.

Enquiry information may be transmitted through email delivery and business email services used to receive and respond to communications.

These services may process information in countries other than the country from which you access our website.

5. Information Sharing

We may share information with authorized personnel, relevant business service providers or other parties where necessary to respond to your enquiry, provide services or meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not intend to sell personal information submitted through our enquiry form.

6. Information Retention

Enquiry information may be retained for the period reasonably necessary to respond to communications, manage relevant business relationships and satisfy applicable legal or recordkeeping requirements.

7. Website Cookies and Technologies

Our initial website does not intentionally use advertising trackers or marketing profiling technologies.

Hosting and security services may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary to provide functionality and protect the website.

8. Information Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse and disclosure.

However, no electronic transmission or information storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Please do not submit passwords, government identification numbers, payment card details or other sensitive information through our general enquiry form.

9. Your Privacy Requests

Subject to applicable law, you may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information you have provided to us.

You may also contact us regarding withdrawal of consent or concerns about the handling of your information.

10. Children's Privacy

Our business enquiry services are intended for adults and business representatives. Children should not submit personal information through our enquiry form.

11. External Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites. Those websites are governed by their own privacy policies and practices.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in website functionality, business practices or applicable legal requirements.
